# Heritage Gallery Report

## Baseline

Only the Sunan Giri profile had a gallery item, with a caption warning that the exact photo location was unconfirmed. Eight profiles had no gallery. Ten `Document/` images were inspected; several are not attributable to a specific Wali Songo site, and the generic stone image has no verified provenance.

## Additions and limits

Licensed site images are registered in the manifest with creator, license, source page, caption, and a location-verification note. They are shown as present-day documentation, not historical evidence. For sites with no verified matching image, the gallery shows a labeled placeholder rather than reassigning a different site's image. Clickable gallery media opens a captioned lightbox.

No Google Drive asset source was connected or accessed for this project.
