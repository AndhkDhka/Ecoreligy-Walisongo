# Product Image Report

## Baseline

Most of 21 product records had no image. Existing image-bearing product entries were a Gresik Batik photo (its depicted maker/site still requires attribution confirmation) and a Pudak illustration. The Wingko image is an illustration and is not used as a photograph of local retail stock.

## Handling

Cards display matching verified media or an explicit placeholder. Regional craft/products are identified separately from items confirmed sold at a site. No price, seller, address, stock, or availability is inferred from an image. Item-specific image credits are provided in the asset manifest.
