# Source Verification Report

## Reviewed public sources

- Gresik tourism/culture office: [Nasi Krawu Gresik](https://disparekrafbudpora.gresikkab.go.id/detailpost/nasi-krawu-gresik), [Kupat Keteg](https://disparekrafbudpora.gresikkab.go.id/detailpost/kupat-keteg), and [Kolak Ayam/Sanggring](https://disparekrafbudpora.gresikkab.go.id/detailpost/kolak-ayam-sanggring). These support their Gresik/regional relation; Kupat Keteg is identified with Giri, while Sanggring is a Gumeno tradition associated in local tradition with Sunan Dalem, a son of Sunan Giri. They are not presented as foods served at the tomb.
- Demak Tourism Office: [Makam Sunan Kalijaga](https://pariwisata.demakkab.go.id/makam-sunan-kalijaga-demak/), for Kadilangu location and recurring local rituals. Event traditions are labeled as local practice.
- Kudus Regency portal: [Makam Sunan Kudus](https://kuduskab.go.id/index.php/80715f88-387c-4125-b602-bb935d28a8e0) for site location and published visitor details; those current hours are dated and subject to reconfirmation.
- Kudus Regency portal: [Museum Jenang](https://kuduskab.go.id/6064c3c5-d7ea-4ce6-b5c6-f2becdcc9ebe) for Jenang Kudus context.
- Cirebon Regency portal: [Desa Wisata Religi Astana](https://arsipweb2022.cirebonkab.go.id/news/2023/08/desa-wisata-religi-astana-gunungjati-kabupaten-cirebon-masuk-75-adwi-2023) for Astana's destination context and regionally offered foods. These are described as village/region items, not tomb-site vendors.
- Lamongan Regency tourism culinary magazine for local dishes: [E-Magazine Kuliner](https://lamongankab.go.id/beranda/documents/disparbud/E-MAGAZINE%20KULINER.pdf).

## Editorial treatment

The existing data's history/source strings remain as-is unless a specific source can be attached. `historical_fact`, `academic_interpretation`, and `local_tradition` are translated to visitor-facing labels. Unverified dates and genealogies remain uncertainty-labeled. General organization-name source lists are not represented as citations for a specific fact.

## Image rights

Commons file page and license/creator credits are listed per image in the asset manifest. File changes are recorded when assets are resized/optimized. No third-party image is treated as public domain without a stated license.
