# Feature Status

## Active

- Five-item responsive primary navigation and light/dark theme.
- Nine Wali Songo destination profiles and details, with existing history/source notes and locally relevant content.
- Leaflet cultural map, nine approximate Wali Songo area markers, location permission, and external map directions.
- Per-destination Guided route with local progress and manual fallback.
- Sunan Giri MindAR image-tracking pilot, camera fallback, and abstract Three.js interpretation.
- General visit etiquette and practical-information caveats.

## Partial

- AR landmark recognition: only one registered image target is available; other destinations rely on Guided text.
- GPS: the map and Guided flow can show the visitor’s device location, but no destination arrival is verified.
- Destination photography: the repository contains site assets, but many profile records have no assigned cover/gallery image.
- Route guidance: turn-by-turn road directions open an external mapping service; there is no in-app routing engine.

## Not available / not claimed

- Spatial AR, automatic building or tombstone recognition, live AR navigation arrows.
- Verified opening hours, entrance prices, facilities, or crowd estimates across all destinations.
- Booking or operator contact.
- AR matching or manual check-in as proof of a physical visit.

## Removed from primary navigation

Digital Passport/tracker, planner/build journey, journey inquiry, standalone Culinary, Products/UMKM, Cultural Discovery, and Events. Their existing deep-link routes and data remain for compatibility during this redesign; they are not offered as primary product modules.
